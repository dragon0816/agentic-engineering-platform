# Market alignment evidence matrix

Owner decision: first produce a 5×5 evidence and knowledge-gap matrix, then investigate individual cells. The rows are nonsignaling testers (CMP180, CMP200, CMW100), signaling testers (CMX500, CMW500), signal generators/analyzers (FSW, SMB), vector network analyzers (ZNA3000, ZNB), and oscilloscopes (MXO, RTO5, RTO6). The columns are wireless communications, aerospace/defense, automotive, AI server, and semiconductor.

The first slice is a read-only, deterministic scan of the selected installed Knowledge Vault's current Raw sections. For each cell it distinguishes a nearby product/market mention within 350 characters, separate product and market passages, and no matching Raw passage. A nearby mention is a discovery lead, never proof of application fit or an option recommendation. Each lead carries exact Raw provenance and a bounded excerpt. Every cell lists the missing market requirements, product/option specifications, and validation needed before a recommendation. No external search result is silently imported into the Vault. AI-server retrieval uses AI/GPU-specific phrases rather than generic data-center or server words.

A dedicated Bridge capability owns the scan and a closed output contract. The Host registers it only for installed Knowledge; its exact read grant is separate from installation. Agent Web resolves a named Knowledge asset and matrix intent deterministically, invokes that capability, and formats the response. Ordinary Knowledge Ask and its grounded answer contract remain unchanged. A shipped Skill explains the evidence levels and the drill-down procedure; it does not execute code or grant access.

Acceptance: 25 cells in stable row/column order; current Raw only; correct source/section references; no affirmative market fit from co-occurrence; no model or credential required; exact Bridge denial remains visible; ordinary Knowledge questions unchanged. Focused contract, scanner, and Web tests precede implementation. A later slice may ask the model to analyze one selected cell against confirmed requirements and options, with human review of any recommendation.
