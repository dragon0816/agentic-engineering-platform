# RS Knowledge Vault --- 1,100 Question Benchmark

> Purpose: evaluate Vault routing, retrieval, evidence coverage,
> evidence-to-claim grounding, reasoning, and knowledge gaps. These
> questions intentionally do **not** contain fabricated golden answers.
> Evidence must come from the RS Knowledge Vault.

## Benchmark composition

-   **Q0001--Q1000:** 5 Market Segments × 4 Product Families × 50
    questions.
-   **Q1001--Q1100:** Product-related technical/foundation knowledge.
-   Matrix-cell mix: 10 Basic, 10 Application, 10 Solution Mapping, 8
    Cross-domain, 7 Competitive, 5 Strategic/Synthesis.

## Evaluation rule

A response is acceptable when it retrieves relevant evidence, evidence
is sufficient, substantive claims are grounded, and the conclusion
reasonably follows. `INSUFFICIENT_KNOWLEDGE` is valid when the Vault
lacks evidence.
